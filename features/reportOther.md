# Reporting Other categories
## Feature Summary
This section helps users create and manage reports for other categories such as life events. 

## Common
Upon reaching this page, all users will see a list of their reports in table view.  This will display the name of the person who created the report, report type, report created date, and a status indicator set by the FSO (unreviewed, under review, processed). Users can filter and search on these criteria. Users will click on the button marked "report life event for myself or others" to open a popup window to fill in the details of their life event.  Upon submitting, the assigned FSO has an action item created for them. Upon submitting, the default staus is unreviewed.

Any user with their internal position marked management will also have access to a button marked "FCL Reporting".

At the top of this page, there will be the following header and text.

## Non - IC Admin Panel
Non-IC users will also have the ability to view all reports in their purview and filter by suborganization.  FSOs and Admins can mark the report as processed or under review upon their review and submission in DISS. 
 
## Workflows
### Reporter
1. The reporter creates the report and it appears in their reports.  The status is set to unreviewed. The assigned FSO gets a task created in their task panel.  The title is "Process [REPORT TYPE] for [REPORTER FULL NAME].

### Processor
The processor is the assigned FSO or any FSO who the assigned FSO reports up through. 
1. The processor can also asccess reports from this report dashboard.  If they wish to process a report from the report dashboard instad of the task panel, they can click on the the report.  Once the report is open, the status dropdown (which is only available to administrators and FSOs) is available. Here the processor can update the status dropdown.  Once the status is set to processed, it is cleared from the assigned FSO's task board.
