# A wiki page for users within a tenancy
## Feature desciption
This wiki provides a place for FSOs and Admins to share relevant documents, links, and posts for their end users.

## Common
The title of this page will be Government Security Homepage
FSOs and Admins can post a wiki post to the entire org or sub organization.  All users will be able to click on the tile and see the full post in popup view. 

On this page there will also be a tile for "Your FSO".  This will display the current user's assigned FSO, as well as their emai.
